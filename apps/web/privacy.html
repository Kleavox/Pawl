POLICY / PRIVACY

Privacy, without ambiguity.

This policy explains what {{SITE_NAME}} processes when you create an account, authenticate through Google or GitHub, create links, share files, or monitor infrastructure. Effective June 7, 2026.

Data we process

  • Account name, email address, authentication provider, and security events.
  • Short-link destinations, settings, access counters, and expiration data.
  • Temporary files, file metadata, download limits, and deletion schedules.
  • Infrastructure metrics, checks, incidents, and enrolled node identifiers.
  • Contact-form names, email addresses, messages, and abuse-prevention signals.

Google and GitHub sign-in

{{SITE_NAME}} requests only basic identity information needed to create and secure an account: an account identifier, verified email address, and profile name. OAuth credentials are used for sign-in and are not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and service providers

Cloudflare Workers, D1, KV, and R2 process application traffic and stored service data. Resend processes transactional email and contact-form messages. Google and GitHub process authentication according to their own policies.

Sensitive actions such as registration, password resets, and guest uploads are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which runs invisibly and processes a limited set of signals to verify that a visitor is human. Cloudflare's handling of this data is described in its Turnstile Privacy Policy Addendum, which supplements Cloudflare's general privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Temporary files are removed when their configured expiration or download limit is reached. Other service records are retained while needed to provide, secure, and troubleshoot the service. Account and related data deletion requests can be submitted through the operator's contact form.

Sharing and security

Personal data is not sold. It is shared only with service providers required to operate {{SITE_NAME}}, when you direct the service to share content, or when disclosure is legally required. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact and changes

Questions, access requests, and deletion requests can be sent through the operator's contact form. Material policy changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
